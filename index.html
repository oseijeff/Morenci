              Maintenance

              A maintenance program built for each well from its equipment and water chemistry, with a record of what was done and what is due. Record one completion per task to start its clock.

              Equipment and parts

              What is installed on each well, how to service it, the parts and consumables to hold, and specifications to issue when buying replacements. Equipment data comes from the 1997 Upper Eagle Creek O&M manual; service intervals combine that manual with the Resilient Well Manual.

              Project report
